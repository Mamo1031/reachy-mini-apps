# 公式モーション集の出典
以下のファイルは Pollen Robotics 公開のデータセット(Apache-2.0)からコピーしたものです。

- https://huggingface.co/datasets/pollen-robotics/reachy-mini-emotions-library (revision 873ae49f0b89114b7e535eff0c1f7560d21d9357)
  - `welcoming1.json` — A welcoming gesture to greet someone.
  - `welcoming2.json` — A friendly welcoming gesture, can mean 'welcome' or 'the pleasure is mine.'
  - `cheerful1.json` — It is like whistling. Use it when someone makes you a proposal that makes you happy.
  - `success1.json` — Use this gesture when you’ve successfully completed a task.
  - `enthusiastic1.json` — A movement to celebrate incredible news.
  - `serenity1.json` — You try to calm down and regain inner peace.
  - `downcast1.json` — A longer movement that shows discouragement or sadness. You can also use it as a sad no
  - `understanding1.json` — You nod to show you’ve understood what your interlocutor said.
  - `understanding2.json` — You nod to show you’ve understood and agree. Can also be used to say yes.
  - `amazed1.json` — When you discover something extraordinary. It could be a new robot, or someone tells you you've been programmed with new abilities. It can also be when you admire what someone has done.
  - `come1.json` — A gesture to invite your interlocutor to come closer.
  - `no_excited1.json` — An animated negative response, for example, when you don’t want to do something but want to explain it playfully.
  - `inquiring2.json` — A lighter questioning gesture than inquiring1. As if you're thinking but need more details.
  - `inquiring3.json` — A fast movement that lets you ask a question.
  - `thoughtful1.json` — You look up as if searching for a new idea, especially in complex or uncertain situations.
